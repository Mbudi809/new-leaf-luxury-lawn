Our Story & Mission

New Leaf Luxury Lawn was founded by a small team of landscaping professionals who saw too many beautiful Johannesburg gardens let down by inconsistent maintenance. We set out to build a service that Northern Suburbs homeowners and businesses could rely on — one appointment at a time, one lawn at a time.

Today, we combine traditional lawn care craftsmanship with modern, low-maintenance turf solutions, so that every client — from a busy family home to a boutique hotel — enjoys a garden that always looks its best, without the weekend effort.

Our mission remains simple: to transform outdoor spaces into pristine, luxurious, and effortlessly maintained landscapes through exceptional lawn care, attention to detail, and reliable service.
